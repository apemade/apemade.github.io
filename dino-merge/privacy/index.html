Dino Merge – Privacy Policy

Ape Made Games · Effective 9 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Dino Merge (package com.apemade.games.mergegames.dinosaurs.dinomerge), published by Ape Made Games ("we", "us"), collects and how it is used.

Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email, location or any account.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects device information, including your advertising ID, to deliver and measure ads.

Information stored on your device

Game progress and settings (such as sound and vibration on/off) are stored locally on your phone. We do not receive them. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game deletes them. Android's own backup may include them in your personal Google backup.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Dino Merge is free and supported by ads. Ads are shown only when you choose to watch one (for example, to get a power-up or a bonus reward). To show and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID (Android)Ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement and, where you allow it, personalized ads
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, IP address-derived approximate region)Ad delivery, fraud prevention and analytics
Ad interactions (views, taps)Measurement, fraud prevention and rewarding you for watched ads

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services. All data is sent over encrypted connections.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).
  • You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the game.

Children

Dino Merge is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the advertising data collected by Google AdMob described above, the game does not share data with third parties.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
